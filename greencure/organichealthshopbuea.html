HISTORY

This business came to me as a passion and with the help of my associate pastor who play a vital rule in the kick starting and Background work of this business. It all started in 2018 with limited product and Capital. By then we were still on a smaller scale, were we supply from home to shops and supermarket. As time passes by, we saw that, the was an increase in demand and need for natural product. so, we started thinking of having a base / a shop which will be the center where people can come for purchasing or locate us.
Presently, the business has expanded wildly and we now supply good in all part of Cameroon be it Yaoundé, Bamenda, Kumba, Limbe, North, West, Douala, etc. We also have machines use for the production of flour, organic Cosmetics product, Dieting, supplement, natural herbs, natural dried fruit etc.; dehydrators machine used to produce natural oil.
